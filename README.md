# Nurse Burnout: Natural Language Processing Analysis

**Python | NLP | Sentiment Analysis | Topic Modeling | Healthcare Analytics**

## Project Overview

This project explores how natural language processing (NLP) can be applied to online discussions about nurse burnout to identify recurring themes, analyze sentiment, and examine the limitations of automated text analysis in healthcare research.

Developed as part of graduate coursework at the University of Colorado Boulder, the project combines computational analysis with qualitative interpretation to investigate how healthcare workers describe workplace stress and burnout.

## Research Objectives

- Analyze sentiment in nurse burnout discussions.
- Identify recurring topics and language patterns.
- Explore how workload, staffing, and workplace conditions are represented in online discussions.
- Compare automated NLP outputs with qualitative interpretations.
- Evaluate the reliability and limitations of NLP methods applied to healthcare-related discussions.

## Data Sources

The project draws on three types of sources:

**1. Reddit — Qualitative Text Data**

Discussions from r/NurseBurnout and r/nursing provide examples of firsthand experiences related to workplace stress, emotional exhaustion, and burnout.

**2. Kaggle — Structured Reference Data**

The [Burnout in Nurses dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shauryapal/burnout-in-nurses/data) provides structured burnout-related indicators to contextualize patterns identified in textual discussions.

**3. Statista — Healthcare Workforce Context**

Published statistics on nurse burnout provide broader context for interpreting the significance of workplace stress and burnout.

These sources serve different analytical purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable datasets.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data processing and analysis
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and preprocessing
- **NLTK / VADER:** Sentiment analysis
- **Scikit-learn:** Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) topic modeling
- **Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab:** Development, execution, and documentation

## Methodology

**1. Data Preparation**

Prepared structured and unstructured data for analysis, including text cleaning and preprocessing.

**2. Sentiment Analysis**

Applied NLP-based sentiment classification to examine emotional tone in discussions related to nurse burnout.

**3. Topic Modeling**

Used LDA topic modeling to explore recurring language patterns and potential themes within the text.

**4. Qualitative Evaluation**

Examined automated outputs alongside contextual interpretations, paying particular attention to cases where sentiment classification might overlook nuance or emotional complexity.

## Key Findings

### 1. Sentiment Analysis

VADER sentiment analysis was applied to 18 Reddit posts discussing nurse burnout.

- **Negative:** 10 posts (55.6%)
- **Positive:** 8 posts (44.4%)
- **Neutral:** 0 posts

Although negative sentiment was slightly more prevalent, qualitative review revealed that some positively classified posts still expressed burnout-related distress.

This highlights an important limitation of automated sentiment classification: positive language does not necessarily indicate a positive workplace experience.

### 2. Topic Modeling

Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) identified two recurring themes:

**Topic 0 — Burnout and Career Decisions**

Prominent terms included `patients`, `help`, `burnout`, `leaving`, and `work`.

This topic suggests a connection between emotional exhaustion, patient care responsibilities, and considerations about leaving the nursing profession.

**Topic 1 — Emotional and Professional Strain**

Prominent terms included `patient`, `care`, `nursing`, `nurse`, `job`, and `feel`.

This topic reflects the emotional demands of nursing and the relationship between professional identity and workplace experiences.

These themes were interpreted qualitatively based on the terms generated by the model.

### 3. Structured Burnout Indicators

A separate Kaggle dataset containing 50 observations was examined to provide quantitative context.

- **Average pre-measurement burnout score:** 69.84
- **Average post-measurement burnout score:** 46.12
- **Average nursing experience:** 7.64 years

The structured dataset complements the text analysis by illustrating how burnout can be examined through numerical indicators as well as personal narratives.

### 4. Model Evaluation and Interpretation

Comparing automated outputs with qualitative interpretations revealed several limitations:

- Sentiment classifications may overlook emotional complexity and contextual meaning.
- Topic modeling can identify recurring terms but requires human interpretation to establish meaningful themes.
- Small, manually curated datasets limit the generalizability of findings.
- Combining structured indicators with unstructured discussions can provide complementary perspectives, although the datasets were analyzed separately.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python-based NLP techniques can support exploratory healthcare workforce research.

The analysis identified recurring themes related to burnout, patient care, professional strain, and potential career departure. It also demonstrated the importance of evaluating automated classifications against contextual interpretations.

The findings support the use of NLP as a complementary analytical tool rather than a substitute for qualitative assessment or validated measures of nurse burnout.

## Limitations

- Online discussions may not represent the broader nursing workforce.
